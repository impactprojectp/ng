About IMPACT PROJECT PNG

Our mission is to open real opportunities for the people of Papua New Guinea through non-refundable financial support.

Our Mission

Fighting inequality of opportunity by funding education, entrepreneurship, agriculture and community initiatives across Papua New Guinea.

Our Vision

A Papua New Guinea where every promising project, regardless of the applicant's means, can find the funding it needs to come to life.

Our Values

Transparency, integrity, and genuine commitment to the communities we serve, from the National Capital District to the most remote provinces.

Our Approach

A rigorous, fair evaluation process that gives priority to projects with real social and economic impact.

What We Fund

IMPACT PROJECT PNG supports a wide range of initiatives across the country.

Education

Scholarships and study grants for students facing financial hardship.

Agriculture & Fishing

Support for small-scale farmers and fishers looking to grow their activity.

Entrepreneurship

Seed funding for small businesses and community initiatives.
