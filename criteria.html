Eligibility Criteria

Please review the conditions below before submitting your application.

Who Can Apply?

Our aid is intended for residents of Papua New Guinea who meet the following conditions.

🇵🇬 Residency

Be a resident of Papua New Guinea with a valid proof of identity (passport, national ID card, or driver's license).

Age

Be at least 18 years old at the time of application. Minors may apply with parental or guardian consent.

Complete File

Submit a fully completed application form with all required supporting information.

Viable Project

Present a clear, realistic project with a defined budget and a measurable expected impact.

Bank Account

Hold a bank account in Papua New Guinea in your name, for the disbursement of funds.

No Duplicate Requests

Not have an active, unresolved application already pending with IMPACT PROJECT PNG.

Eligible Project Categories

We support a variety of sectors, prioritizing projects with strong community impact.

Education / Scholarship

Tuition fees, school supplies, or vocational training.

Agriculture

Farming equipment, seeds, livestock, or land development.

Small Business

Start-up or growth capital for local businesses.

Health

Community health initiatives and access to care.

Craftsmanship

Tools and materials for artisans and craftspeople.

Fishing

Equipment and boats for small-scale fishers.
